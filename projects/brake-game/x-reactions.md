# X reactions: 尊界 V800 brake pedal bracket incident (collected 2026-10-10, UTC+8)

Source: the `x` MCP (search_posts_all ×6, get_news ×1). Window: Oct 7–10, 2026. Quotes are copied verbatim from post text, and each one has its URL. Lines marked *paraphrase* are my summaries, not quotes.

## Context (X News story 2108104710091616649)
https://x.com/i/trending/2108104710091616649: "Maextro V800 Brake Bracket Fails in Repeated Emergency Tests". Dongchedi tested 3 cars from 100 km/h. The plastic pedal bracket broke at the same spot on all three. Stopping distance went from 38 m to 140 m. JAC fell 10% on Oct 8. The company offered free upgrades and said there had been no real-world failures.

Details that came up a lot (not independently verified):
- Car 1 broke on its 3rd full stop, car 2 on its 4th, and car 3 (bought only to verify) on its 2nd: https://x.com/shanghaidaily/status/2108087506826911793
- Pedal force at fracture was reported as about 1612 N, compared with a commonly cited 2000 N national reference: https://x.com/WeiJiang194454/status/2108451205433434148 , https://x.com/LorenzoSun7z/status/2108147052391841952
- Material was said to be PP+GF40 glass-fibre plastic. People noted a single-layer rib versus 2–3 layers on sibling models, plus a "熔接痕" (weld line): https://x.com/Gaoshouz2026/status/2108135190556295423 , https://x.com/mcudirector/status/2108106136339808598
- Testers stopped the car with the P gear: https://x.com/SonicAIWizard/status/2108039932602437808 , https://x.com/CharlesXBoy/status/2108038707211338052
- JAC closed limit-down at 24.72, −10.01%: https://x.com/ZhengJacks64752/status/2108196986214395971 , https://x.com/whyyoutouzhele/status/2108145378205770135

## Overall sentiment
- Mostly mockery and disbelief, focused on the gap between the price and luxury gadgets on one side and a cheap safety part on the other. "Three cars" and fridge/coffee are the core memes.
- There is a large secondary argument between Huawei fans and Dongchedi critics. That fight is political and tribal, so the game avoids it.
- English-language coverage is mostly straight news (CnEVPost, CarNewsChina, Shanghai Daily, China Daily, Yicai).
